EMBERCRAWL - BETA (please read me)
==================================

Thank you for testing Embercrawl! It's a turn-based dungeon crawl: descend thirty floors of a
buried observatory, defeat the Beacon Warden at the bottom, and rekindle the Beacon.

This is a beta. Things may break, and sound isn't in the game yet.


STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
- Zip download: unzip it anywhere (right-click > Extract All), open the folder, and double-click
  Embercrawl.exe. Nothing is installed.
- Portable .exe: double-click it. It takes a few seconds to start each time.
- Installer: run it; it adds Embercrawl to the Start menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the game isn't signed yet. Click
"More info", then "Run anyway".


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Press H in the game (or "How to play" on the title screen) for the basics and every key.
Keys can be changed in Settings. Hover the mouse over anything in the dungeon to see what it is.


YOUR SAVES
----------
Characters are saved after every turn, in %APPDATA%\Embercrawl\Saves (the title screen's
"Open saves folder" button opens it). Deleting or moving the game itself never touches them.
The game also keeps a few backups of each character.

To move a character to another PC, use Manage > "Export to a file" on the title screen, then
"Import character" on the other PC.


REPORTING A PROBLEM
-------------------
1. In the game, press Esc and choose "Copy a bug report". It copies the details the developer
   needs (version, floor, what just happened) - paste it into your message.
2. Say what you were doing and what went wrong.
3. If it's about a particular character, attach its file: Manage > "Export to a file".

Things the developer would love to hear about:
- Where you died, and whether it felt fair.
- Anything confusing, or anything you looked for and couldn't find.
- How far you got, and how long it took.
